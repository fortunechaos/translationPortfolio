Plurality - the experience of having more than one distinct consciousness, person(a), or identity sharing a single body (The Plural Association)

Instructions: Click anywhere to build the system, click on members to cycle through their aspects and build a soundscape, click on the title to create a random soundscape

The process of creating this work has been:
1. Commitment: Did I want to share something this personal in an academic space? It wouldn't be the first time that I came out for the sake of art I wanted to pursue.
2. Counting and Recounting: Listing all of my selves I had to go back through the process of our collective development. It is easy to lose track of someone, we are not always all equally present.
3. Expression: Some of my selves had a clear association for how they would sound in the soundscape, others involved a more abstract approach.
4. Presentation
