Duct Tape

I am lost in the sea of ambiguous silver and gray that is the Shaw's Plaza parking lot, searching for a car that my family has owned for quite a short time in the scheme of things (though over a year now) when the woman approaches me asking for help. For a while I can't make heads or tails of her request, do I know anything about landline phones? Well not really. We're standing in a slate gray drizzle and I'm more than ready to wish her good luck and make my escape when she starts to actually explain what the problem is. An unlocked wire box on the side of her house and an entirely unreachable customer service office while a storm rolls in. I can provide the perfect answer after all. Duct tape.
